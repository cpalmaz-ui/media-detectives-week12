MISSION 1 · THE HEADLINE BATTLE · 25–40 MIN

Same imagined event. Different frames.

Read the headlines. Both are hypothetical and neither proves what occurred.

HEADLINE A

Angry protesters disrupt city streets.

HEADLINE B

Citizens demand action during a peaceful demonstration.

1. Which headline uses words that frame demonstrators more negatively?

Explain aloud or in the chat: Which two words influence the reader's perceptions? What important information is missing from both headlines?

Reflection prompt: Does biased framing necessarily mean that the factual event described is false? Explain the difference between perspective and verified evidence.
